Appearance

AMOLED dark background

Turns YouTube's dark-theme page background pure black, with near-black menus and dialogs for contrast. Enable YouTube's dark theme first.

Filters

Block ads

Strips ad breaks from player data, blocks known ad hosts, and hides feed, menu and player promotions.

Filter Shorts

Hides Shorts shelves and tiles across Home, search, subscriptions and channel pages.

Force HDR

Advertises HDR display support so YouTube serves HDR streams. Requires HDR to be enabled in your operating system.

Focus mode

Focus mode

Hides comments, related videos, live chat, playlists, player cards, promos and end screens, and switches to a square, compact layout. Home opens with just the search bar and a button to reveal recommendations; search hides recommendation shelves. Applies instantly.

Advanced

Aggressive ad skipping

Mute and fast-forward short ads that cannot be skipped. Long or server-stitched ads are left at normal speed to avoid skipping the real video.

Pin highest HDR quality

Selects the best available HDR resolution automatically. Turn this off to respect YouTube's Auto quality setting.

Show HDR badge

Displays a small HDR badge in the player when an HDR source is detected.

Convert Shorts links

Rewrites /shorts/ links to normal /watch?v= pages. Turn off to leave direct Shorts links untouched.

How it works

  • Network rules block known ad hosts and ad video requests before they leave the browser.
  • Player scrubbing removes ad placements from YouTube's player responses before its code reads them.
  • DOM filtering hides feed, search, menu and player promotions, with a small fallback that skips ads the player still starts.
  • HDR enforcement advertises HDR display support and optionally pins the best HDR stream that the video offers.
  • Shorts filtering hides Shorts shelves and tiles, and converts direct Shorts links into normal watch pages.
  • AMOLED background overrides YouTube's dark-theme page background with pure black while keeping menus and controls readable.
  • Focus mode hides comments, related content, live chat, playlists, player cards, promos and end screens, switches to a square, compact layout, and adds a Home button to reveal recommendations only when you ask for them.
HDR playback needs an HDR-capable display with HDR enabled in Windows or macOS, plus browser/GPU support. Forcing HDR on an SDR display can make colors look washed out.
YouTube changes its internals often. Some server-stitched ads may still appear briefly; the fallback mutes and fast-forwards short ad clips. If playback breaks after an update, disable aggressive ad skipping first.
